## What landed

Each session gains a `waiting` list, derived from its main transcript by four detectors:
- D1: an unanswered structured question;
- D2: a closing prose question left idle, shipped enabled at the owner's choice;
- D3: permission refusals, read from `toolDenialKind` only (auto-mode-blocked, user-rejected and permission-rule);
- D4: the plan-gate approval.

An item clears on a message the owner actually typed; compaction summaries and the exact tool-use interrupt marker don't count. Items age out after 48 hours, and each session shows five at most, with an overflow count. The Overview shows them in a `--human` panel that ignores the session filter. `PARSER_VERSION` moved 7 → 8.

## What is worth carrying forward

**CR-009-1 was a real-data defect that no fixture caught.** Two record kinds that Claude Code writes itself (compaction summaries, and the interrupt marker written right after a rejection) counted as the owner being present. That cleared every user-rejected refusal a millisecond after it was written. The round-2 reviewer ran the corrected predicate over the owner's 24 real transcripts, and the false owner-presence records fell from 10 to 0. When a detector keys off "the owner was here", check it on real transcripts, not just fixtures.
